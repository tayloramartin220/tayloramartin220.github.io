Budget App

A personal finance dashboard for spending, net worth, investments, debt and savings goals, in as many currencies as you hold money in.

  • Every account, debt and goal keeps its own currency, with totals in the one you choose
  • Debt payoff and savings projections, with and without extra payments
  • Bank imports from Excel or CSV, with data stored only on your device
Open live demo Project page on GitHub

Nomad Notes

A travel planner for long trips: flights, stays, budget, entry rules and stay limits in one place.

  • Keeps what you've paid, committed to and only estimated separate
  • Tracks stay limits for any passport, including rolling 90-in-180 rules
  • Flags refund deadlines and nights with nowhere booked
